One Tile Kingdom

One tile→A KINGDOM

You are the lord or lady of one small patch of land in a fantasy realm. Raise your own house, plant wheat and grow a village, step by step. It might become a market town, or a castle with a kingdom around it.

  • Your home is free to raise. Upgrade it from Cottage to Manor, Keep and Castle, and pick your house colours.
  • Land and wheat: claim tiles, plant wheat, and let villagers harvest it. A windmill turns it into flour.
  • The village: cottages bring villagers. Shops, stalls, taverns and carts sell for you. A smithy, chapel and wizard tower add perks.
  • Upgrades and decrees: spend coins on upgrades for farming, trade and your realm, and fulfil royal decrees for bonus coins. Keep an eye on the sky for a dragon.
Drag with the Hand tool (or right-drag) to pan. Scroll to zoom. WASD moves the camera, 1–9 pick tools, Space pauses.
