One Tile Village

0 days→1000 DAYS

You start with one patch of grass and a small hooded farmer. Buy land, plant wheat, sell the harvest and grow a village. Keep it going for 1000 days.

  • Land extends your island one tile at a time.
  • Wheat ripens in 4 days. Harvest it by clicking, or let villagers do it.
  • Shops, stalls and carts sell for you. A windmill turns wheat into pricey flour.
  • Houses bring new villagers. Trees, wells and paths add charm, and charm lifts every price.
Drag with the Hand tool (or right-drag) to pan. Scroll to zoom. WASD moves the camera, 1–9 pick tools, Space pauses.
